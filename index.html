    FAQ

    Questions people ask before booking.

    Do I need to be home?

    Not always. If the items are outside or in an unlocked garage, leave a note in your booking and we'll text you a photo when it's done. We do need someone there to approve the price for indoor jobs.

    Do I have to move things to the curb?

    No. We pick up from wherever the junk is: basement, attic, upstairs bedroom, backyard. Stairs are included unless it's a heavy appliance or hot tub.

    How do you figure out the price?

    By how much of our 15-yard trailer your items fill, plus a few item fees for things like mattresses and fridges. We show you the price on-site before we load anything.

    What happens to my stuff?

    Usable furniture and household goods go to local donation centers. Metal and electronics go to recycling. Only what's left goes to the transfer station.

    How do I pay?

    After the job is done. We take card, cash, and mobile payments. There's no charge to book or cancel.
